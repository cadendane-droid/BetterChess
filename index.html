    How BetterChess works

    No check, no checkmate. You win by capturing the enemy king, so you can walk into danger. The board warns you when your king can be hit. There is no castling and no en passant.

    The pieces

    The board

    ◎
    Portals. Two linked pairs in the middle of the board (same colour = linked). Finish a move on a portal and you come out of its twin. If an enemy is standing on the twin, it is destroyed. If a friend is there, you stay put.
    ▣
    Supply crates. Every 5 moves a crate drops onto an empty square in the middle four ranks (at most 3 on the board). Move a piece onto it to pick it up.
